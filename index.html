Dispatch and communication software for community responders.

Dynamic Systems is developed and operated by SY Systems LLC. We build and run call-intake, dispatch and team-alerting platforms for community volunteer and emergency-response organizations in Brooklyn, New York.

What we do

Call intake & dispatch

A hotline and dispatch console that logs incoming calls, creates incidents and assigns responders.

Responder alerts

Real-time alerts to volunteer responders through the mobile app, push notifications and voice calls.

Caller updates

Status updates to the people who asked for help — for example, that a responder is on the way.

Privacy

The platforms we operate are used by member organizations to coordinate assistance requests. Phone numbers and call details are used only to handle and follow up on those requests, are never sold, and are shared only with the responding organization. Messages are sent only in connection with a request for assistance; recipients can reply STOP to opt out of text messages. Questions about your data can be sent to the email address above.
